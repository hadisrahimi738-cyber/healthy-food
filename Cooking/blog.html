Pasta

Ingredients
Olive oil – 2 tbsp
Garlic – 2 cloves, minced
Cherry tomatoes – 1 cup, halved
Salt – to taste
Black pepper – to taste
Parmesan cheese – for topping
Fresh basil – few leaves

Instructions:
Boil pasta until al dente. Sauté garlic in olive oil, add tomatoes and cook a few minutes. Toss pasta with sauce, season with salt and pepper, then top with Parmesan and basil before serving. Enjoy your meal!

Chicken Salad

Ingredients
Chicken breast – 1 piece
Lettuce – 2 cups
Tomato – 1
Cucumber – 1
Olive oil – 1 tbsp
Salt – to taste
Black pepper – to taste

Instructions:
Cook the chicken and cut it into small pieces. Mix the chicken with lettuce, tomato and cucumber. Add olive oil, salt and black pepper. Mix well and enjoy your salad!

Fruit Smoothie

Ingredients
Banana – 1
Strawberries – 1 cup
Milk – 1 cup
Honey – 1 tbsp

Instructions:
Add all the ingredients to a blender. Blend until smooth and creamy. Pour into a glass and enjoy!
